# Momo v3.1 - Sales policy retrieval hardening

Regression targets found during the v3.0 live-agent QA:

1. `O meu pagamento falhou. Fico logo sem Momo?` -> mention the 7-day grace period and that service remains active during it.
2. `Se eu pagar depois de o widget ser suspenso, tenho de pedir para o reativarem?` -> say reactivation is automatic after payment is regularized.
3. `Existe fidelização?` -> no commitment, plus the roughly three-month recommendation as non-mandatory.
4. `Se cancelar hoje, devolvem-me os dias que faltam?` -> no prorated refund; service remains active until the paid billing period ends. Do not add unnecessary legal boilerplate.
