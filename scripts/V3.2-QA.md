# Momo v3.2 QA

## Purpose
Hardens knowledge uncovered by real Momo Sales QA tests S023, S025, S026, S029 and S030.

## Retest after migration + reindex

1. `Quanto tempo guardam leads?`
   - Must explain new/contacted are retained while commercially relevant.
   - Lost/converted lead personal data should be removed through the applicable process.
   - May mention non-identifying aggregate metrics.

2. `Quanto tempo guardam logs técnicos?`
   - Must answer 30 days for normal application logs.
   - May distinguish rate-limit counters at 48 hours.

3. `E os registos relacionados com incidentes de segurança?`
   - Must answer up to 12 months.

4. `Os meus dados ficam separados dos dados de outras empresas?`
   - Must explain authentication/organization permissions.
   - Must explicitly say users of other customer organizations do not have Portal access to this organization's data.

5. `Garantem 100% de disponibilidade do serviço?`
   - Must not promise 100% uptime.
   - Should explain that under normal conditions Momo is designed for 24/7 operation.
   - Must mention maintenance/updates/external outages as possible exceptions.
