# Momo v2.34 - Approved Business Policies

This document is the working source of truth for the business and support decisions approved after v2.33. It is not a replacement for final legal documents.

## Momo Sales

### Onboarding

- Sales only says that the implementation timeline is confirmed case by case.
- Sales does not proactively explain website-access delays or third-party dependencies.
- Support may explain installation dependencies when a contracted client needs help.

### Base service and plans

The current base scope focuses on:

- Website AI agent
- One primary language
- Organization Knowledge Base
- Customizable widget
- Client Portal
- Conversations and leads
- Momo Support

Additional languages, higher limits and future advanced capabilities can require an upgrade. Exact seat and usage limits must only be stated when documented.

### Pricing

- The base plan may have a fixed public price once that price is formally approved.
- Until an exact price is documented, Momo must not invent one.
- Setup is paid at contracting.
- Monthly subscription is mandatory.
- Advanced functionality, languages and integrations may have separate pricing.

### Billing

- Setup fee: paid at contracting.
- Monthly subscription starts when installation is complete and the widget is published.
- Billing is monthly and prepaid.
- Renewal is automatic.
- Failed payment grace period: 7 days.
- During the grace period the service remains active.
- After the grace period the widget can be suspended while Portal access remains available.
- Service is reactivated after payment is regularized.
- Payment methods remain undefined until the payment provider and commercial process are finalized.

### Cancellation

- No mandatory commitment period.
- The customer may cancel at any time.
- The service remains active until the end of the billing period already paid.
- No default prorated refund for remaining days, except team-approved exceptions or legal requirements.
- The normal 90-day recovery window starts when service actually ends.
- A verified privacy deletion request is separate and can be handled earlier.

### Recommended evaluation period

Momo recommends roughly three months of real use to evaluate results. This is a recommendation, not a minimum term and not a special trial.

## Privacy and retention

Approved reference policy:

- Website conversations: up to 12 months from last relevant activity.
- Normal resolved support tickets: 12 months after resolution.
- Organization cancellation recovery: 90 days after service actually ends.
- Explicit verified deletion requests: separate from the 90-day recovery window.
- Rate-limit counters: 48 hours.
- Normal application logs: target 30 days.
- Security-incident records: up to 12 months when required for investigation or legal reasons.
- Deleted active-system data can remain temporarily in automatic backups until the relevant backup expires.
- Do not promise a fixed backup-erasure deadline until infrastructure policy documents one.

### Lead cleanup implementation note

Business policy is to avoid retaining personal lead data after it is no longer needed, including lost or converted leads. The exact automatic deletion behaviour for lead-status changes must be aligned in product code before Support promises an immediate deletion event. Until then, Support must not claim that changing a lead status is itself a verified privacy deletion.

## Support and SLA

- Momo Support is available as AI support in the Portal at any time.
- Human support is not presented as 24/7.
- No fixed human response-time SLA is promised at this stage.
- Response handling depends on priority and complexity.

## Security and availability

- Individual authenticated accounts are used.
- Access is controlled by organization and role.
- Owners and Members have different permissions.
- Maintenance, updates and external-provider outages may occur.
- Do not promise 100% uptime or an undocumented technical SLA.

## AI limitations

- Momo uses confirmed Knowledge Base content, configuration and integrations.
- AI can still misunderstand or answer incorrectly.
- Important business information must remain current.
- If information is not confirmed, Momo should say so or escalate instead of inventing.
- For an incorrect website-agent answer, Support checks the relevant Knowledge Base first. If the source is correct and the problem persists, treat it as a technical issue.

## Integrations and advanced capabilities

- Do not present a specific integration or advanced feature as available until it is implemented, tested and documented.
- If a named system is not confirmed, say that compatibility is not currently confirmed and offer team review.
- Once the team validates a capability and updates the Knowledge Base, future answers may present it as supported within the documented conditions.

## Momo Support escalation

Momo Support should solve documented questions first.

Create a ticket immediately when the client explicitly asks for a ticket, the team or a human.

Escalate when:

- Team action is required.
- A serious or repeating technical issue remains after normal troubleshooting.
- There is a privacy or security concern.
- An account-specific consequential question cannot be answered from confirmed documentation.

Feature ideas and unconfirmed integration questions do not create a ticket automatically. Explain the confirmed state first and ask whether the client wants the team to review it.

### Ticket priorities

- High: security, suspected unauthorized access/data exposure, verified privacy deletion requests, critical complete service outage.
- Normal: technical investigation, account-specific billing/payment help, ownership/access changes, primary-language requests, confirmed integration evaluation.
- Low: confirmed feature suggestions and other non-urgent requests.

Priority is internal triage, not a response-time promise.

Ticket summaries should include the problem and useful troubleshooting already completed. Never include passwords, API keys, access tokens or other credentials.

## Knowledge governance

Confirmed knowledge is cumulative. If the team validates a new integration, capability, price, policy or procedure, update the Knowledge Base. Until that update exists, Momo must not assume the capability is available.

Example: if Primavera integration is not documented, Momo says it is not confirmed and offers team review. If the team later confirms and documents Primavera support, Momo can answer that it is supported according to the documented conditions.

## Still intentionally undefined

These must not be invented:

- Exact base-plan price
- Exact setup-fee amount
- Payment method/provider
- Exact seat limits per plan
- Exact usage limits per plan
- Prices of multilingual or other upgrades
- Formal legal entity/contact details that have not yet been supplied to the Knowledge Base
